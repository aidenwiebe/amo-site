Consuming over 100 hours of my time as every instrument is recorded by me and the final edit and mix is quite extremely meticulous, Forest City Gallery was too passive to stamp their logo on the already-completed cover art.  I suppose an instrumental hip hop-inspired jam band doesn't seem important enough.  Aimless in hindsight.  

Featuring performances by Nathan Nykor, Grant Graham, Bret Wade, Truly KADE, Ricky Chee, Philip Gurrey, Patrick Linardic, Amanda Movio, SXXN, Caleb Brown, Ruby, Miguel Antonio Thompson, and myself.
