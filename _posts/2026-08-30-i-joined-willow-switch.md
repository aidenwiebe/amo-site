You can tell by my confusion writing these credits that I am not authorized to release this.  Unfortunately for authority, we're all anarchists.  Willow Switch is currently in the studio, though they'll have a tough time topping what has replaced Dark Side of the Moon as my favourite album of all time.  Their lyrics tap into the collective unconsciousness, their performances are unpolished enough to bleed authenticity, but not so much to where they sound amateur.  This is music to accompany life;;;; I can't not fall in love: [The Eternal Dance of Pearl and Hermes: Side Z](http://apolmus.org/pearlandhermes_side_z.mp3)
